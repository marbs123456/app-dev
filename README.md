My Favorite Movie: The Maze Runner
Why This Movie Is My Favorite

The Maze Runner is one of the movies I really enjoyed because the story is full of mystery and action. From the start, it makes you curious about the Maze and why the characters were placed inside it. The storyline keeps building up, which makes it exciting to watch.

Characters I Like

Thomas – I like how brave he is and how he always takes the lead when everyone else is unsure.

Newt – He’s calm, understanding, and one of the most reliable characters in the group.

Minho – Strong and fast, and he adds energy to the team.

What Makes It Interesting

The survival theme and the mystery behind the Maze make the movie intense and unpredictable.

Movie Details

Genre: Sci-Fi, Action, Adventure

Based on the novel by: James Dashner

Main theme: Finding the truth behind the Maze

Favorite line: “If you ain’t scared… you ain’t human.”

Overall Thoughts

This movie stands out to me because it mixes action with a mystery that slowly unfolds. It shows how teamwork and trust can help people survive even the toughest situations. That’s why The Maze Runner is one of the movies I always recommend.
